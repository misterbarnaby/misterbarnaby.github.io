Barnaby privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Barnaby is a personal app with one user: its owner. It runs on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, Barnaby uses the Google Calendar API to create events in calendars the owner owns. It only writes an event after the owner has seen it and agreed.

What it does with that data

  • The data stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google account.
  • It is not sold, shared or sent to anyone else.
  • It is not used to train any AI model. Barnaby's AI runs locally on the owner's computer.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking.

Removing access

The owner can remove Barnaby's access at any time in their Google account (Security, Third-party connections).

Barnaby's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
